About the company

Creators of refined
living & iconic spaces.

FORMA Living develops timeless homes across India — elegance, privacy and comfort, quietly composed.

Who we are

Mumbai-born. Quietly ambitious.

Founded in 2014, FORMA began with a single Worli floorplate and a refusal to sell “views” that didn’t exist. Today we develop boutique towers, gated villa communities and penthouses in Mumbai, Pune and Alibaug.

We combine investment discipline with high-design sophistication — from land strategy to handover — so each home feels like a landmark, not a listing.

“Integrity is the foundation of every decision — from partners to paint.”

Company principle
