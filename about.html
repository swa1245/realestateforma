Practice

Architecture for a bespoke life

We do not subscribe to a single style. Like a curator, FORMA matches visionary architects and landscape designers to the identity of each site — Worli glass, Baner gardens, Alibaug courts.

Every residence begins on paper: site notes, hand sketches, then measured drawings. Renders come last — when the light path is already proven.

“Every space begins with how morning light enters — not with a marketing render.”
